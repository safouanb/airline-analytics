## 1. Business Background

**Domain:** Airline Industry - Passenger Satisfaction

**Context:** 
The airline industry is highly competitive, and passenger satisfaction is crucial for customer retention, brand reputation, and profitability. Understanding what drives satisfaction can help airlines:
- Improve service quality
- Reduce customer churn
- Increase customer lifetime value
- Optimize resource allocation

---

## 2. Business Objectives

### Primary Objective
**Use data mining techniques to identify key drivers of customer satisfaction and develop predictive models that can inform strategic business decisions to improve the airline's business model.**

### Secondary Objectives
1. Identify service areas requiring immediate improvement
2. Understand differences between customer segments (loyal vs. disloyal, business vs. personal, class differences)
3. Quantify the impact of operational factors (delays, flight distance) on satisfaction
4. Build predictive models that can accurately predict customer satisfaction

---

## 3. Research Questions

### Main Research Question
**How can we use data mining to identify the key drivers of customer satisfaction and predict satisfaction levels to improve the airline's business model?**

### Specific Research Questions
1. **What factors most strongly predict passenger satisfaction?**
   - Which service attributes have the highest impact?
   - Are there interaction effects between services?

2. **Can we accurately predict passenger satisfaction?**
   - What is the predictive power of available features?
   - Which model performs best?

3. **Which passenger segments are most at risk of dissatisfaction?**
   - Are there demographic or travel characteristic patterns?
   - How can we target interventions?

4. **What is the relationship between service quality and satisfaction?**
   - Which services need the most improvement?
   - What is the priority order for service enhancements?

5. **How do operational factors (delays, class, travel type) affect satisfaction?**
   - What is the impact of delays on satisfaction?
   - Do different passenger types have different expectations?

---

## 4. Success Criteria

### Technical Success Criteria
- Model accuracy > 85% in predicting customer satisfaction
- Successfully identify top 5-10 most important satisfaction drivers
- Achieve precision and recall > 80% for both satisfied and dissatisfied classes
- Model performs well on test set, indicating good generalization

### Business Success Criteria
- Provide clear, prioritized recommendations for service improvement
- Identify at least 3-5 high-impact improvement opportunities
- Successfully identify distinct customer segments with different satisfaction drivers
- Insights directly relate to improving revenue, retention, or operational efficiency
- Recommendations are feasible and can be implemented by the airline

## 6. Constraints and Assumptions

- Historical patterns will continue
